# Welcome

The **Computer Science Atlas** is a connected, subject-oriented guide to computer science. It is designed to be readable as a website and editable as ordinary Markdown in Obsidian.

## Explore

- [[Authoring-Guide|Open the authoring guide]] to see the Markdown conventions used in this project.
- [[../Computer-Science/index|Visit the Computer Science landing page]].

> [!tip] Follow links as you read
> Pages link to related concepts. Use the search box, graph, and backlinks to find another route through the material.

This is demonstration content for the project scaffold. The complete subject taxonomy and any migration of existing notes are future work.
